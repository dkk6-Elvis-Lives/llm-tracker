## Notes and caveats

- **Secondary sources.** Vendor primary docs cover most cells, but several dates and prices are only available from third parties: Grok 4.3's release date (xAI never published one — cloud partner cards give [Oracle 2026-05-01](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/releasenotes/generative-ai/xAI-grok-4-3.htm), [Google Cloud 2026-05-27](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/partner-models/grok/grok-4-3), [AWS 2026-06-15](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-card-xai-grok-4-3.html)); Qwen3.5/3.6/3.7 announcement dates ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/alibaba-unveils-new-qwen35-model-agentic-ai-era-2026-02-16/)); Kimi K3's launch date ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/16/moonshots-kimi-k3-pushes-chinese-ai-into-fable-level-territory/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-moonshot-unveils-worlds-largest-open-ai-model-closing-us-rivals-2026-07-17/)); and open-weights inference prices for Llama, Gemma and older Qwen/DeepSeek, which have no vendor list price and are quoted from a host ([DeepInfra](https://deepinfra.com/pricing)). Cohere legacy prices come from [Cohere's pricing FAQ](https://cohere.com/pricing) and, for two rows, [pricepertoken](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/provider/cohere).
- **Hidden pricing tiers.** Listed rates are standard, non-cached, on-demand. Batch discounts (50% at [Anthropic](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/about-claude/pricing), [Google](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) and [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/pricing/api/); 20% at [xAI](https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing)), prompt-cache read/write multipliers, long-context surcharges (Gemini 3.1 Pro, Qwen tiers, GPT-5.6 above 272K, Grok 4.5 above 200K), Anthropic "Fast mode", and xAI priority (2x) tiers all change effective cost materially.
- **"Current" is uncertain.** Vendors rarely mark supersession explicitly. Rows are labelled from deprecation/retirement schedules and from whether a model is still listed and priced; models with announced future shutdown dates (e.g. GPT-5 family, 2026-12-11) are shown as deprecated even though they still serve traffic today.
- **Tokenizers differ.** Anthropic notes Opus 4.7+ uses a new tokenizer producing ~30% more tokens for the same text, so per-token prices are not directly comparable across vendors.
